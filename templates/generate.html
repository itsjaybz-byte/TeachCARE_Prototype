Generate Optimized Schedule

TeachCARE evaluates possible teacher assignments against the implemented eligibility constraints before passing valid candidates to the optimization model.

1. Candidate Eligibility Screening

Before schedule generation, possible teacher–curriculum requirement–period combinations are filtered according to four eligibility conditions.

Subject Qualification

The teacher must be qualified to teach the required subject.

Educational Attainment

The teacher must satisfy the minimum educational attainment requirement associated with the subject.

Educational-Level Eligibility

The teacher must be eligible to teach at the educational level of the section, such as JHS or SHS.

Teacher Availability

The teacher must be available during the specific scheduling period.

2. Binary Optimization Model

Eligible candidate assignments are represented using binary decision variables. The optimizer applies the curriculum requirements and hard scheduling constraints while minimizing total workload deviation.

xt,r,p ∈ {0,1}
Wt = total assigned contact periods
Dt = |Wt − 6|
Objective: minimize Σ Dt

Where t represents a teacher, r represents a curriculum requirement, and p represents a schedule period.

3. Generate Optimized Schedule

Eligible candidates are passed to the binary optimization model to generate a schedule that satisfies curriculum requirements and hard scheduling constraints while minimizing workload deviation from the target workload.

TeachCARE Computational Workflow
Input Data → Eligibility Filtering → Binary Optimization → Generated Schedule → Workload Analysis → Independent Validation

This workflow represents the implemented computational framework used by the TeachCARE prototype.
