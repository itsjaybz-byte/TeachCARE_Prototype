Research Input

Independent validation separates hard scheduling feasibility from workload quality. A schedule may satisfy all required curriculum, qualification, eligibility, availability, and scheduling constraints while still exhibiting workload imbalance. The workload assessment therefore provides a quantitative basis for evaluating the quality of the generated teacher assignment.
